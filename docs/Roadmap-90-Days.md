# 90-Day Roadmap – Integrated Release

This is a gated plan, not a promise that every product will launch within 90 days. Licensing, venue readiness and the eight-week pilot may extend the calendar.

## Days 1–14 – Validate and protect
- Interview at least 20 parents; test outcomes, schedule, location, price and trust.
- Confirm Egyptian legal classification, licensing and venue requirements with relevant professionals/authorities.
- Finalize I CAN objectives, challenge templates, portfolio fields and baseline rubric.
- Appoint safeguarding lead; prepare consent, privacy, collection, complaint, emergency and incident procedures.
- Identify qualified first-aid training partner; practical first aid requires appropriate qualification.
**Gate:** viable legal path, validated problem, safeguarding plan and realistic budget.

## Days 15–28 – Build the minimum viable experience
- Secure suitable hourly/day venue or partner.
- Recruit and screen facilitators; train on LIFE360, child protection, inclusion, privacy and risk assessment.
- Prepare eight anchor challenges and simple paper/controlled digital Achievement Passport.
- Prepare science/maker materials, digital citizenship scenarios and career discovery stations.
- Rehearse sessions; host discovery workshop and test paid reservations.
- Run Parent Academy as one introductory workshop.
**Gate:** venue, staff, documents, activities, permissions, safety controls and minimum paid enrollment ready.

## Days 29–56 – Pilot delivery (calendar may extend)
Deliver eight-week I CAN: two sessions weekly. Run one anchor challenge per week; collect artifact, observation and reflection. Record baseline, midpoint and endline evidence. Send factual parent updates and conduct weekly safety/quality reviews.
**Gate:** never compress learning or safeguarding steps to fit the calendar.

## Days 57–70 – Evaluate and refine
Review learning evidence, attendance, completion, child voice, parent satisfaction, complaints, actual revenue, variable costs, staff time, acquisition and contribution margin. Identify strongest challenges and extensions; revise method, staffing and price assumptions.
**Gate:** evidence of educational value, safe delivery, demand and viable unit economics.

## Days 71–90 – Controlled next release
Open a second cohort only if gates are met. Hold a small Future Expo with separate media consent and event safety controls. Schedule a Parent Academy workshop. Test one school/community partnership. Define platform requirements from observed workflows; no expensive custom development without validated scope and funding.
**Gate:** demonstrated team, venue and safeguarding capacity.

## Product release sequence
| Priority | Product | Initial approach |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Challenges | Pilot immediately; paper templates |
| P0 | Achievement Passport | Manual evidence-based MVP |
| P1 | Leadership, Digital Citizenship, Science, Career Discovery | Integrate into I CAN |
| P1 | First Aid & Safety | Awareness in pilot; practical modules via qualified provider |
| P1 | Parent Academy | One workshop and home guidance |
| P2 | Future Expo | Small showcase after consent/event controls |
| P3 | Digital Platform | Requirements/prototype after manual validation |

## Weekly scorecard
Learning evidence; attendance; challenge completion; engagement/inclusion; parent feedback; safety/near misses; staff hours; cash position; leads/conversion; decisions, owners and due dates.

## Pilot targets
Internal targets: 10 paid enrollments, ≥85% completion, ≥85% parent satisfaction, ≥50% renewal/progression intent, portfolio coverage for all children subject to permissions, evidence of progress in at least two practiced skills per child, ≥90% delivery adherence, zero serious incidents attributable to preventable control failure. These are management targets, not benchmarks or guarantees.
