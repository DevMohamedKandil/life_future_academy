# 90-Day Launch Roadmap

Planning sequence only; actual timing depends on licensing, venue, staffing, and demand.

## Days 1–14: Validate and define
- Check name availability and trademark implications.
- Obtain professional guidance on legal activity classification and requirements.
- Finalize learning outcomes, session plans, safeguarding, privacy, consent, and incident procedures.
- Interview at least 20 parents; document desired outcomes, objections, schedule, price signals, and trust concerns.
- Prepare a landing page/waitlist only after privacy requirements are considered.

**Gate:** Validated problem, viable legal path, no unresolved critical safeguarding issue.

## Days 15–28: Prepare
- Identify an appropriate room for hourly/day rental or partnership; inspect and obtain written terms.
- Recruit facilitators and complete role-appropriate screening.
- Train using the Trainer Manual.
- Procure approved materials/devices.
- Prepare parent guide, workbook, baseline assessment, and portfolio templates.
- Rehearse activities and host an introductory workshop.

**Gate:** Venue, team, materials, parent documents, emergency process, and rehearsal ready.

## Days 29–56: Pilot
- Target ten paid children.
- Confirm permissions and authorized pickup.
- Deliver two sessions weekly for eight weeks.
- Record attendance and observable learning evidence.
- Send factual parent updates.
- Review safety and delivery after each session; log improvements.

**Gate:** Complete without relaxing safeguarding requirements for commercial targets.

## Days 57–70: Evaluate
Analyze attendance, completion, satisfaction, renewal intent, portfolio evidence, parent/facilitator feedback, actual revenue, costs, refunds, and staff hours. Revise objectives, timing, staffing, and price assumptions.

**Gate:** Evidence of demand, learning value, safe delivery, and acceptable unit economics.

## Days 71–90: Controlled next step
Open a second cohort only if gates are met; test one partnership channel; improve parent follow-up; publish only evidence-supported claims with permissions; maintain curriculum change log and next-quarter plan.

**Gate:** Do not scale beyond demonstrated staff, venue, and safeguarding capacity.

| Individual progress evidence | ≥2 skills per child |
| Delivery adherence | ≥90% |
| Serious preventable safety incidents | 0 |

These are management targets, not market benchmarks or guarantees.
