# Integrated Products, Parent Academy, Expo & Digital Platform

## Product portfolio and release gates
| Product | Educational role | Initial delivery | Scale gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Challenges | Apply learning to authentic problems | Weekly facilitator-led challenge | Safe, repeatable delivery and learning evidence |
| Achievement Passport | Make progress visible | Controlled paper/digital template | Stable evidence fields and parent usefulness |
| Leadership | Practice teamwork and responsibility | Rotating roles in every cohort | Facilitator consistency and child feedback |
| Digital Citizenship | Safer, thoughtful technology use | Scenario cards and supervised tools | Reviewed content and privacy controls |
| First Aid & Safety | Prevention and help-seeking | Awareness activities; specialist practical session | Qualified provider and approved content |
| Science | Inquiry, observation and evidence | Safe, pre-approved experiments | Risk assessments, materials and staff competence |
| Career Discovery | Broaden awareness of fields and roles | Rotating role-play stations | Diverse, stereotype-aware content |
| Parent Academy | Extend family learning partnership | One practical workshop per cohort | Feedback and useful home practice |
| Future Expo | Celebrate learning and explain projects | Small controlled showcase | Event safety, permissions and venue |
| Digital Platform | Improve parent visibility and operations | Manual MVP first | Validated workflows, privacy/security and budget |

## Parent Academy starter modules
- Encourage independence without doing tasks for the child.
- Build reading and conversation routines at home.
- Support curiosity and interests without pressure.
- Respond constructively to mistakes and frustration.
- Teach digital safety, privacy and AI awareness.
- Understand the Achievement Passport and give useful feedback.

## Future Expo format
Child-led displays, demonstrations, short presentations, challenge stations and portfolio reflections. Offer varied participation modes. Use controlled guest access, staff supervision, event risk assessment, emergency plan and separate media permissions. Avoid unnecessary identifying details.

## Digital Platform – phased product brief

### Phase 0: Manual MVP
Secure templates for attendance, challenge logs, portfolio and parent reports. Validate what trainers and parents actually use.

### Phase 1: Admin + Trainer
Cohort/session management, attendance, challenge library, rubric observations, artifact metadata, permissions and audit trail.

### Phase 2: Parent Portal
Authenticated access to the parent's own child's progress, approved artifacts, reports, schedule and academy announcements.

### Phase 3: Learning resources
Age-appropriate home activities and curated resources. No open social network, child-to-child direct messaging, public profiles or advertising to children.

## Non-functional requirements
Privacy by design; least-privilege role access; secure authentication; encryption in transit and at rest; audit logging; backups; retention/deletion workflows; accessibility; parent consent management; incident response; data minimization. Obtain specialist security and legal review before production.

## Explicit exclusions for first release
No AI profiling of children, automated talent scoring, public ranking, location tracking, behavioral advertising, unnecessary child accounts or uploading identifiable child media to third-party AI systems.
