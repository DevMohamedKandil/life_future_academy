# I CAN – Integrated Pilot Curriculum

**LIFE360 v2.1 | Ages 9–12 | 8 weeks | 16 sessions | 10 children | 90 minutes/session**

## Program promise
Children practice independence, planning, financial awareness, safety, design, scientific inquiry, critical thinking, digital citizenship, leadership, communication and career exploration. These are learning targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Session rhythm
5 min welcome/check-in; 10 min concept/text/demo; 10 min modeling; 25 min guided practice; 25 min challenge/project; 10 min reflection and portfolio evidence; 5 min close/cleanup. Adjust for accessibility, transitions and safety.

## Week-by-week plan
| Week | Theme | Session 1 | Session 2 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity & independence | Interests and personal learning goal | Responsibility and workspace organization | Intro card, checklist, writing sample |
| 2 | Self-management & leadership | Time map and priorities | Team roles, listening and respectful decisions | Weekly plan, team agreement |
| 3 | Financial & life skills | Needs, wants and fictional budget | Mini-market simulation and receipt writing | Budget sheet and trade-off explanation |
| 4 | Safety & first-aid awareness | Spot hazards and prevent risk | Recognize need for help and alert trusted adult | Safety map and help-seeking script |
| 5 | Science & making | Question, prediction and safe experiment | Design/build/test/improve a model | Experiment log and prototype |
| 6 | Thinking & challenge solving | Patterns, facts, opinions and evidence | Team challenge: define, ideate, choose, test | Challenge card and team report |
| 7 | Digital citizenship & careers | Privacy, respectful behavior, misinformation and AI errors | Career discovery stations | Digital safety pledge and exploration reflection |
| 8 | Capstone & expo | Build Future Ready City | Future Expo, presentations and portfolio review | Project artifacts and reflection |

## Future Ready City
Teams create a safe miniature city including a community need, fictional budget, environmentally thoughtful feature, safety plan, designed model, team roles, written labels and oral presentation. Each child explains one decision and one improvement.

## Literacy integrated throughout
Read instructions, short science texts, schedules, labels and scenario cards. Write plans, checklists, budgets, experiment logs, captions, reports and reflections. Practice listening, discussion, respectful disagreement and presentation.

## Challenge and portfolio cadence
One anchor challenge weekly, plus optional micro-challenges. Capture baseline in week 1, at least one meaningful artifact/observation weekly, midpoint review in week 4 and final review in week 8. Photos/audio/video require separate permission; written artifacts can replace media.

## Career exploration
Use rotating role-play stations and stories, not aptitude tests. Include varied fields without gender, class or ability stereotypes. Record interests in the child's own words and revisit later.

## First Aid boundary
Pilot lessons focus on prevention, recognizing danger and seeking adult help. Practical first-aid techniques are delivered only by a suitably qualified instructor using approved age-appropriate content.

## Assessment
Use the LIFE360 five-level rubric against each child's baseline. Collect observation + artifact + child explanation where possible. Do not rank children. Parent reports describe observed evidence, context, support needed and next practice step.
