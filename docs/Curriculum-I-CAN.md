# I CAN – Pilot Curriculum

**LIFE360 Method v2.0 | Ages 9–12 | 8 weeks | 16 sessions | 10 children | 90 minutes/session**

## Outcomes
Practice independence, time planning, basic budgeting, safety awareness and help-seeking, design and iteration, problem-solving, beginner coding concepts, reading, writing, communication, and talent exploration. These are learning targets, not guarantees.

## Suggested 90-minute flow
5 min welcome/check-in; 10 concept/story/demo; 10 facilitator modeling; 30 hands-on practice; 20 team challenge; 10 reflection/evidence; 5 close/cleanup. Adjust for safe transitions and individual needs.

## Week 1 – Independence
1. **Who am I?** Identity card, interests, five sentences about self, listening agreement. Evidence: card and writing sample.
2. **My space, my responsibility.** Organize a workspace, follow safe instructions, create a checklist. Home link: organize one safe personal area with parent agreement.

## Week 2 – Self-management
3. **Where does my time go?** Read a fictional daily schedule, sort fixed/flexible activities, write a balanced-day paragraph.
4. **Plan and do.** Break tasks into steps, prioritize, create a weekly plan, discuss changes.

## Week 3 – Financial awareness
5. **Needs, wants, and a budget.** Sort examples, read sample prices, allocate a fictional budget, explain trade-offs.
6. **Mini market.** Role-play buyer/seller with fictional tokens, compare prices, complete a receipt. No real-money exchange or financial-data collection.

## Week 4 – Safety
7. **Spot the hazard.** Use illustrated home/school scenes, map hazards, choose avoid/tell adult/follow rule. No dangerous demonstrations.
8. **Ask for help and prepare.** Identify trusted adults with parent involvement, practice help-seeking language, plan a hypothetical safe meeting point. No frightening drills or public disclosure of sensitive family details.

## Week 5 – Maker skills
9. **Design and build.** Sketch and build using facilitator-approved safe materials.
10. **Test and improve.** Define a fair test, record observations, receive feedback, make one improvement. Avoid sharp tools, hot glue, mains electricity, and choking hazards.

## Week 6 – Thinking skills
11. **Patterns, evidence, and opinions.** Pattern puzzles, short informational text, fact/opinion, evidence-based inference.
12. **Solve the challenge.** Define a team problem, generate two alternatives, compare by criteria, justify a decision, write a brief team report.

## Week 7 – Future skills
13. **Think like a programmer.** Unplugged sequencing; introduce sequence, condition, repetition; create a simple Scratch project. Adult-managed devices only.
14. **Data and AI awareness.** Classify synthetic data, discuss errors, verify fictional AI outputs. Never upload child images, voices, or personal data to third-party services.

## Week 8 – Capstone
15. **Build Future Ready City.** Teams plan a miniature city, assign roles, map/budget, build safe models, label and describe their work.
16. **Showcase and reflect.** Team presentations, audience questions, personal reflection, portfolio review, optional parent showcase with permissions.

## Integrated literacy
Read instructions, stories, labels, schedules, and informational passages. Write plans, checklists, receipts, captions, explanations, and reflections. Practice listening, dialogue, and presentation. No arbitrary book quota.

## Talent exploration
Observe repeated experiences in science/engineering, creative arts, social/leadership, analytical/logical, language/expressive, and physical/movement domains. Record interest, initiative, persistence, creativity, learning from mistakes, progress, and desire to continue. Never label from one activity.

## Assessment
Use the five-level LIFE360 mastery rubric. Record baseline, formative evidence, and end reflection. Compare each child primarily with their own prior performance; do not publicly rank children.
