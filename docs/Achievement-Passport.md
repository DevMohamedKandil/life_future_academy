# LIFE360 Achievement Passport – Data & Learning Specification

## Purpose
A cumulative, child-centered record of learning evidence, not a points race, public leaderboard, IQ score or formal academic transcript.

## Record structure
1. Child identifier and cohort (use internal ID where practical).
2. Parent/guardian contact and permissions stored separately with restricted access.
3. Baseline goals and child-selected interests.
4. Eight LIFE360 dimensions with observable skill statements.
5. Challenge records: date, challenge ID, role, artifact, observation, support level and reflection.
6. Reading/writing/communication samples.
7. Talent and career exploration notes in the child's own words.
8. Parent-supported activities and feedback.
9. Midpoint/endline review and next-step suggestions.
10. Consent metadata for any media.

## Evidence standards
Prefer artifacts, facilitator observations and child explanations. State context and assistance level. Record a strength and next practice step. Do not infer personality, diagnosis, intelligence or future occupation.

## Minimum viable release
Start with a controlled template (paper or access-restricted document). Review with parents and facilitators before digitizing. A custom platform is not a prerequisite for the pilot.

## Privacy and access
Collect only necessary data; define lawful basis, purpose, role-based access, secure storage, retention/deletion and correction process. Parents see only their child's records. No public profiles, cross-family sharing or media publication without separate permission. Review Egyptian data-protection requirements with qualified counsel.
