# LIFE360 Challenge System – Operating Specification

## Purpose
A reusable challenge library turns the LIFE360 learning cycle into visible, assessable experiences. Challenges are collaborative but must capture each child's own contribution.

## Challenge card template
ID, title, version, age band, duration, scenario, child-friendly problem, target dimensions, objectives, success criteria, constraints, materials, preparation, accessibility adaptations, team roles, literacy task, facilitator prompts, safety assessment, deliverable, evidence, rubric, reflection and optional home extension.

## Pilot challenge bank
1. **Build a Bridge:** design a bridge from approved safe materials and test with a light standardized load.
2. **Plan a Community Day:** allocate a fictional budget, sequence tasks and communicate a plan.
3. **Safer School Route:** inspect an illustrated fictional map, identify hazards and propose safer choices.
4. **Clean Water Inquiry:** explore a safe facilitator-prepared filtration model; never drink experiment output.
5. **Invent for Reuse:** design a useful object from clean reusable materials.
6. **Spot the Unsupported Claim:** compare fictional information cards, identify evidence and explain uncertainty.
7. **Digital Kindness Scenario:** choose safe responses to fictional online situations.
8. **Future Ready City:** integrated capstone with roles, budget, science/environment, safety, model and presentation.

## Quality and scoring
Do not score children against each other. Assess evidence against the five-level LIFE360 rubric and document context/support. Planning, testing, teamwork, communication and reflection count alongside the final object.

## Safety
Every challenge must be approved by the facilitator lead before delivery. No hazardous tools/materials, dangerous competition, personal disclosure, public exposure or unsupervised internet use. Stop when controls fail.
